🌱 Smart Farming Tips

🌾 Crop Rotation

Rotating crops improves soil fertility and reduces pest attacks.

More Information:
  • Plant different crops each season.
  • Helps maintain soil nutrients.
  • Reduces pest and disease buildup.
  • Example: Rice → Legumes → Vegetables.

💧 Smart Irrigation

Use drip irrigation to save water and improve crop growth.

More Information:
  • Drip irrigation reduces water waste.
  • Water goes directly to plant roots.
  • Improves crop yield and growth.
  • Useful in water-scarce regions.

🧪 Disease Monitoring

Regularly check plant leaves to detect diseases early.

More Information:
  • Check leaves weekly for spots or discoloration.
  • Use AI leaf detection tools.
  • Remove infected plants quickly.
  • Apply recommended pesticides if needed.
